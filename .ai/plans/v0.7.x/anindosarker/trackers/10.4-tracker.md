## Attention

- D65 names `ST_TableBody` and `localization.select`; the spec (1.6.2) places the group around the `<table>` in `ST_Table` with `aria-label={localization.toggleSelectRow}`, and the build follows the spec. Aria `ST_Table.tsx` differs by `onChange` and is added to `aria-diff.json`; base and radix are byte-identical.
- The play tests have no table instance in the story, so `rowSelection` is asserted through the rows' `data-selected` (only row 3) plus the radios' checked state. ArrowDown is held, then released: Radix checks the focused radio only while an arrow key is down and moves focus in a timeout, so an instant press-and-release does not select in the test runner.
- The group uses `className="block"` rather than `contents`, so the radiogroup role stays in the accessibility tree in every browser.
