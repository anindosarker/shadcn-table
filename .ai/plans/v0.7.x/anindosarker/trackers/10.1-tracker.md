## Attention

- The five aria menu anchors render as `<a>` without `href` and without a role inside `<Pressable isDisabled>` (D26 kept literally, review fix): Pressable skips its role check for `a` and `isDisabled` skips the focusable check; the element keeps `aria-hidden`, `tabIndex={-1}` and `inert`. The hook still names it `spanProps`.
- `ST_TableLayout` changed in the three bases (one line, passes `data-st-full-screen`), although spec 1.6.4 says it does not: the layout picks `onKeyDown` and `tabIndex` from `containerProps` instead of spreading them, so without it the capture listener never matches inside the built-in layout.
- The element `onKeyDown` in `useST_FullScreenContainer` now skips editable targets too; before, Escape in the search field also left full screen, which the 1.2 goal forbids.
- No localization key names the loading state, so the aria progress bar's `aria-label` is the English "Loading", overridable through `stLinearProgressProps`; a new key would widen `ST_Localization` and every locale.
- Aria `ST_SelectCheckbox`, `ST_FilterCheckbox` and the grouping value lose their hover tooltips (D42 addendum kept them; spec 10.1 replaces them): the text is the control's `aria-label`, or `title` on the span.
- A disabled tooltip child (pagination, clear buttons) renders without the tooltip and remounts once when it becomes enabled.
- Aria `ST_FilterTextField` wraps the multi-select chip list in `TagGroupContext.Provider` with an `aria-label`, a fix beyond spec 1.6.2's operations table, needed for the zero-warnings goal.
- `useST_FullScreenContainer`'s capture listener matches any `[data-st-full-screen]` on the page, not this table's container: with two tables in full screen state, Escape inside table B also leaves table A's full screen. Kept as is; a container ref would scope it if the owner wants.
