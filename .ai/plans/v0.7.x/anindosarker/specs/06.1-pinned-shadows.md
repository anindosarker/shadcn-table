# 06.1: pinned shadows

## 1. Architecture

### 1.1 Overview

Pinned columns read as under the scrolled columns in light mode and scrolled text bleeds through them: the pinned cell gets `opacity-[0.97]` and its `::before` backdrop `bg-background/97`, and the pinned-edge shadow is `inset`, so in light mode it darkens the pinned cell's own edge while in dark mode the same edge is lighter than the background and reads as a highlight on top (F62). The bottom toolbar uses the same inset form. D47 keeps the D32 `color-mix` colors and turns the shape around: pinned cells opaque, shadows cast outward onto the scrolled content. Sub-spec of 06; workstream T1 (index, Run plan): branch `v0.7/pinned-shadows`, worktree `../shadcn-react-table.worktrees/pinned-shadows`, from `claude/review`. All three bases; the three files are byte-identical across bases except the aria `style.utils.ts` line offset (+33).

### 1.2 Goals

- A pinned column shows no scrolled text through it in either theme; its shadow falls on the first scrolled column, in LTR and RTL (D47) (D31)
- The bottom toolbar's shadow falls upward on the table body, not inward (D47)
- The sticky header row keeps its outward `shadow-[4px_0_8px_...]`; no change (D47)
- `check:tailwind` green; `grep -n "inset\]" style.utils.ts ST_BottomToolbar.tsx` prints nothing in any base (D16)
- Acceptance: `pnpm check:tailwind` exits 0 for base, radix and aria and `grep -rn "opacity-\[0.97\]\|bg-background/97\|_inset\]" packages/shadcn-table/registry/*/components/ui/shadcn-table/shared/utils/style.utils.ts packages/shadcn-table/registry/*/components/ui/shadcn-table/components/toolbar/ST_BottomToolbar.tsx` prints nothing; judged: `reference/06-capture.mjs` captures of `pinned-columns` and `toolbar` per base, light and dark, beside the committed `reference/06-*` baseline, three judges on "pinned column over the scrolled columns, no text bleed, toolbar shadow on the table body; dark mode unchanged in character" (P6)
