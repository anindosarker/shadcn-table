### Diagnosis

- (a) Page index after select all, `select-all-page-mode`: not reproduced. Select all, unselect,

  of range; core's auto reset goes to page 0. No fix; plays guard the label.
- (b) Select all stuck indeterminate: follows from (a). Core `getIsAllPageRowsSelected` reads
  `getPaginatedRowModel().flatRows`, which keeps the pinned rows (only `getCenterRows` drops
  them). On page 2 the page rows are unselected, so the box is indeterminate and a click

- (c) Page index after `Clear selection`: follows from (a); `Clear selection` writes the
  selection and the pins only. No fix; a play asserts the label.
- (d) Order and label, `select-all-all-mode`: `getST_Rows` in `shared/utils/row.utils.ts`
  puts the top-pinned rows kept from other pages (core `getTopRows`, `keepPinnedRows`) before
  the page rows, so Jackson, Nora and Owen come first; the pinned ids themselves are in row
  order. `components/toolbar/ST_TablePagination.tsx` labels the page only. Fix: under
  `select-sticky` the kept rows go above or below the page rows by row model position; under
  `select-*` the label range covers the kept pinned rows; the selection subscription sorts a
  batch of newly pinned ids by row model position (sorted tables, custom row ids, the
  `select-top` and `select-bottom` order); `ST_TableBody` takes the sticky stacking indexes
  from the rows it shows (the kept rows had index -1).
- (e) Sticky pinned rows over the head, `row-pinning-select-sticky-mode`: select all pins 50
  of 50; `46 of 50` is four more clicks at the select-all box, each landing on a pinned row
  and unselecting it. Cause in `components/body/ST_TableBodyRow.tsx`: a sticky pinned row has
  a `top` and a `bottom` inset; with 50 pinned rows the `bottom` inset (the pinned rows below
  times the row height) is taller than the scrollport and pulls the row up to the table top,
  its containing block, over the head (not sticky in this story), and the `top` inset (0
  without a sticky head) does not stop it. Fix: the row drops its `bottom` inset when the
  pinned stack (pinned rows times the row height, plus the head and the sticky footer) is
  taller than the container's `clientHeight`; it then keeps its flow position below the head
  and sticks at the `top` inset on scroll. The `top` inset is unchanged (the head height only
  under a sticky head or full screen), so a stack that fits behaves as before. Not a
  z-index: the head's stacking context (its `opacity`) is set in `ST_TableHead`, outside this
  lane, and `style.utils.ts` styles only the cells inside it.
- Ruling 9: `getST_SelectAllHandler` owns select all (the checkbox, the keyboard handler and
  `Clear selection`); on unselect under `select-*` it also calls
  `setRowPinning({ top: [], bottom: [] })`. The subscription keeps the per-row pins.
