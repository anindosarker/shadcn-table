Title:

Show HN: Shadcn-table – a table component library for shadcn/ui

Body:

I made a table component library for shadcn/ui.

I got tired of reimplementing table features from scratch in every project. Sorting, filtering, pagination, pinning, selection, the same work every time. With this it's one install, and the table follows your theme and looks like the rest of your app.

I spent a lot of time on how it looks and feels. I went through the tables people like, Notion, Linear, Airtable and the like, and designed one you get from the get-go instead of something you have to style into shape.

It's built on TanStack Table and shadcn/ui.

Demo: https://www.shadcn-table.com
GitHub: https://github.com/anindosarker/shadcn-table

Feel free to try it out. It's open source and contributions are welcome.
