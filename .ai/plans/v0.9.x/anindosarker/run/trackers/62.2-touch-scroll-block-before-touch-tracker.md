Notes:
- The registration before the touch was already in place at 7f95957d: 4f8c8c5c (spec 62) keeps one non-passive window touchmove listener while a drag source is mounted, the dnd-kit TouchSensor.setup pattern; Q126 describes the code before it. This milestone moves that listener to the capture phase (an element under the finger that stops touchmove no longer lets the page scroll after the hold) and registers it only for sources that can drag (`enabled` option; header cells pass `mayDrag`).
- What Chromium emulation proves: the play checks that pointerdown adds no window touchmove listener, that a touchmove before the hold is not prevented (a swipe scrolls), and that after the hold it is prevented even when an element on the way stops it; the CDP run shows the five spec 62 behaviours. What it cannot prove: WebKit on iOS ignoring a listener added after touchstart, and whether iOS Safari honours the window listener's preventDefault once the hold has passed; Chromium honours a late listener, so no emulation fails on that WebKit rule.
- The real-device check of D108 (iPhone or iPad Safari: header hold drag keeps the page still, quick header swipe scrolls) stays owed to the release that carries this.
