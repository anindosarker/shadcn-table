This example turns on most of what shadcn-table offers in one client-side table. It sets `enableRowSelection`, `enableColumnOrdering`, `enableColumnPinning`, `enableGrouping`, `enableFacetedValues` and `enableRowActions`, renders a panel under each row with `renderDetailPanel`, and replaces the top toolbar through `renderTopToolbar` with a layout built from exported parts such as `ST_GlobalFilterTextField`, `ST_FilterChips`, `ST_SortPopover` and `ST_ViewSettingsButton`. The footer uses `paginationDisplayMode: 'pages'`, and the selection banner sits at the bottom with `positionToolbarAlertBanner`.

Use it as a reference for how the options combine, or as a starting point you trim down to what your screen needs: delete the options you do not want and the rest keeps working. Every part renders with the shadcn/ui components copied into your project, so a change to a button or an input in `components/ui` shows up here too.

All data stays in the browser. For server-side sorting, filtering and pagination, see the [Remote&nbsp;Data&nbsp;Example](/docs/examples/remote) or the [React-Query Example](/docs/examples/react-query).
