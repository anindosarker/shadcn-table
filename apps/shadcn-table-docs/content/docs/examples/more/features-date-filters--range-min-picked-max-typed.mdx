### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click the Joined filter box and click 3 November 2025 in the popup. The table shows `Ben` and `Cara` at once.
2. Click `Max` and type 11 November 2025. The rows do not change while you type.
3. Press Enter. `Dan` returns.

</TryIt>
