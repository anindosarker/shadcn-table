### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Drag the right edge of `Age`. `Age` widens and `Info` widens with it.
2. Drag the right edge of `Name`. `First Name` and `Last Name` widen together.
3. Double-click the edge of `Age`. `Age` goes back to its first width.

</TryIt>
