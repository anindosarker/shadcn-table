### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `Start resize`, then `Group by age`. Rows group, with no `Expand all`.
2. Click `End resize`. The `Expand all` button appears in the header.
3. Click `Ungroup`. The `Expand all` button goes away.

</TryIt>
