### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click the date box under `Joined` and type 10 October 2025. All six rows stay while you type.
2. Press Enter.
3. The table shows `Ben`, `Cara`, `Dan` and `Eve`, who joined after that day.

</TryIt>
