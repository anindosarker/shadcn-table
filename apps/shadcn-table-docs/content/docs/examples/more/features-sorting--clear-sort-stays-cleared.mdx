### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `Name` and choose `Sort ascending`. The rows read Luna, Milo, Otis.
2. Click `Name` again and choose `Clear sort`. The rows read Milo, Luna, Otis.
3. Click `Re-render`. The rows stay Milo, Luna, Otis.

</TryIt>
