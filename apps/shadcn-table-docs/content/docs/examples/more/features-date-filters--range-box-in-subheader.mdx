### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click the Joined filter box. A popup opens with `Min` and `Max` and a calendar for each.
2. Type 10 October 2025 in `Min` and press Enter. `Ann` and `Finn` hide.
3. Click the box's clear button. Both ends empty and all six rows return.

</TryIt>
