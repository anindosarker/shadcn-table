### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click the arrow on a row with `Age` over 50. City, State, Zip and Phone show.
2. Look at a row with `Age` 50 or under. It has no arrow.
3. Click the double arrow (`Expand all`). Rows 50 or under show `No details available`.

</TryIt>
