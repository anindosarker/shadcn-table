### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Look at the `State` filter. It shows a chevron and no `Clear filter` button.
2. Click into the `State` filter and press the down arrow. The state list opens.
3. Pick `AZ` and press Escape. `AZ` shows in the field, with `Clear filter`.

</TryIt>
