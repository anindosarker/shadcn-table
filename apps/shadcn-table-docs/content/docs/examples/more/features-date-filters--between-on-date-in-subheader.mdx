### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click the Joined filter box. A popup opens with `Min` and `Max`.
2. Type 11 November 2025 in `Max` and press Enter. `Eve` hides.
3. Click 3 November 2025 in the calendar. The rows change at once.

</TryIt>
