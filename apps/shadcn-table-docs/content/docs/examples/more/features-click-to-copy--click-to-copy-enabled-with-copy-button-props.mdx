### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click a cell in each column. Its text is copied to the clipboard.
2. Press `Tab`, then `Shift+Tab` back to the cell. Its tooltip says `Copied to clipboard`.
3. Click the empty space to the right of an `Email` address. It copies too: the button fills the cell.

</TryIt>
