### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `State` and choose `Group by State`. Rows gather under one row per state.
2. Click the arrow on a state row. Its people show under it.
3. Drag `Gender` by its handle onto `Drop to group by Gender`. Gender groups too.
4. Click the `State` chip in the `Grouped by` banner. State grouping is removed.

</TryIt>
