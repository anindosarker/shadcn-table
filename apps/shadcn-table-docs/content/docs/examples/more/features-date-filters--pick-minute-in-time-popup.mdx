### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Read the table. It shows `Ben`, `Cara` and `Eve`, who joined after 14:00.
2. Click the clock button in the box under `Joined` and click `30` in the minutes.
3. The box says 14:30 and `Ben`, who joined at 14:15, is gone.

</TryIt>
