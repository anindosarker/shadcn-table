### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click the date box under `Joined` and type 10 October 2025. All six rows stay.
2. Click the calendar button and pick 10 October 2025. The table shows `Ben`, `Cara`, `Dan` and `Eve` at once.

</TryIt>
