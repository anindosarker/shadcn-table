### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `Filter` and choose `Gender`. Its options are the genders in the data.
2. Choose `Age` from `Filter`. A slider sets the age range.
3. Choose `First Name` from `Filter` and open its list. The names show reversed in capitals.

</TryIt>
