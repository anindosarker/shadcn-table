### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `Show/Hide columns` and drag `City` by its grip onto `First Name`.
2. Look at the table. `City` is now the first column.
3. Click `Reset order`. The columns go back to their first order.
4. Close the menu and drag the `State` header onto `Last Name`. `State` moves there.

</TryIt>
