### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click the Joined filter box. A popup opens with `Min` and `Max`.
2. Type 10 October 2025, 12:00 AM in `Min` and press Enter. `Ann` and `Finn` hide.
3. Click the box's clear button. All six rows return.

</TryIt>
