## Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `State` and choose `Group by State`. Rows gather under one row per state.
2. Look at `Gender` on a state row. It shows `Count:` and the group's size in red.
3. Click the arrow on a state row. Its people show under it with their gender.

</TryIt>
