## Try it

<TryIt>

1. Look at both tables. `Deactivate`, `Activate` and `Contact` are greyed out.
2. Tick a row in the first table. Its top buttons turn on; the bottom ones stay off.
3. Tick a row in the second table. Its bottom buttons turn on.
4. Untick the row in the first table. Its top buttons are greyed out again.

</TryIt>
