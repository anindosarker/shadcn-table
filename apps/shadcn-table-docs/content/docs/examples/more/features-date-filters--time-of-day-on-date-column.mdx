### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Read the filter under `Joined`. It says `Filter Mode: Greater Than`.
2. `Ben`, `Cara` and `Eve` remain. They joined after 12:00.
3. `Ann`, `Dan` and `Finn` are hidden: they joined in the morning.

</TryIt>
