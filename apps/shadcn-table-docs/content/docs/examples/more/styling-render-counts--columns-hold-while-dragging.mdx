### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `Start column drag`, then `Group by age`. Rows group, with no `Expand all`.
2. Click `End column drag`. The `Expand all` button appears in the header.
3. Click `Ungroup`, then `Start row drag`. The rows ungroup.
4. Click `Group by age`, then `End row drag`. `Expand all` appears on the second click.

</TryIt>
