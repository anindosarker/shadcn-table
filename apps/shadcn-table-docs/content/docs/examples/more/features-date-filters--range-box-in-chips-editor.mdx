### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `Filter` and choose `Joined`. The editor shows one box, `Pick a date`.
2. Open the box, type 10 October 2025 in `Min` and press Enter. `Ann` and `Finn` hide.
3. Click 3 November 2025 in the calendar. Only `Ben` and `Cara` remain, at once.
4. Close the popup and click the box's clear button. All six rows return.

</TryIt>
