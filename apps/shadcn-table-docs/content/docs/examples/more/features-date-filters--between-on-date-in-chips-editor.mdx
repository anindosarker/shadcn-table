### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `Filter` and choose `Joined`. The editor shows one box, `Pick a date`.
2. Open the box, type 11 November 2025 in `Max` and press Enter. `Eve` hides: an end alone filters.
3. Type 10 October 2025 in `Min` and press Enter. `Ben`, `Cara` and `Dan` remain.

</TryIt>
