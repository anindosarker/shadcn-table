### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `Row Actions` on a row. The menu shows `Edit`, `Share` and `Delete`.
2. Click `Toggle density`. The rows get taller.
3. Click `Due` and choose `Sort ascending`. The earliest dates come first.

</TryIt>
