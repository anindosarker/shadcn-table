## Try it

<TryIt>

1. Tick the box on the first row. `1 of 15 row(s) selected` shows below the rows.
2. Tick the box on the second row. The banner reads `2 of 15 row(s) selected`.
3. Click `Clear selection`. The banner goes away.

</TryIt>
