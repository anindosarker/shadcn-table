### Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click `First Name`. Its menu has no `Group by First Name`.
2. Drag `Last Name` by its handle onto `Drop to group by Last Name`. Rows group.
3. Drag `First Name` by its handle. No drop zone for grouping shows.

</TryIt>
