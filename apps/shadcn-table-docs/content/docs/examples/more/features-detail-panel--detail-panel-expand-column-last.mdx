## Try it

<TryIt>

1. Click the arrow at the end of row 1. A panel with City, State, Zip and Phone opens.
2. Click the double arrow at the right of the header (`Expand all`). All five panels open.
3. Click the arrow at the end of row 1 again. Its panel closes.

</TryIt>
